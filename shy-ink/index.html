Alcuni pezzi sono coperti da altri: aspettano che quelli sopra se ne vadano, quindi parti da quelli liberi.Every picture is covered by a sheet of film made of pieces. Grab a lifted corner, drag it and watch the film curl away: the illustration appears underneath, with the little secret hidden inside it. Some pieces are covered by others and wait until the ones on top are gone, so start from the free ones.
